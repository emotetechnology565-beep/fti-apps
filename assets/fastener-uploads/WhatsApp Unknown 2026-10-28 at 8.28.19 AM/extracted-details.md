2. Obtain the original certificate and verify its status and applicable business scope before claiming manufacturing certification.
3. Confirm the address/postal code from an original record.
4. Clarify the flat-product dimension axis and provide width × thickness × length combinations.
5. Confirm unusual grade spellings and grade availability by size.
6. Provide bolt property classes, product standards, thread pitches and coating specifications.
7. Provide roof-sheet substrate, coating, profile-specific coverage and supporting performance ratings; reconcile alternative widths with the metric range.
8. Supply prices, stock quantities, standard-grade MOQs, lead times, packing weights, contact details and commercial terms; none are supplied here.
